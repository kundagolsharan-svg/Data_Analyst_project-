# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Report
**Dataset:** Sample Retail Sales Data (Jan 2024 – Dec 2025) · 2,025 raw rows → 2,018 cleaned rows
**Prepared with:** `python/01_data_cleaning_and_eda.py`

---

## 1. Data Cleaning Summary

| Issue | Action Taken | Rows Affected |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate rows | Dropped exact duplicates | 7 |
| Missing values (Quantity, UnitPrice, Discount, Profit) | Imputed with the **median for that product Category** | ~130 cells |
| Missing values (Region, ShipMode) | Imputed with the **column mode** (most frequent value) | ~60 cells |
| Inconsistent text casing (`"north"`, `"OFFICE SUPPLIES"`) | Standardized to Title Case | ~250 cells |
| Stray whitespace in names | Stripped | ~200 cells |
| Mixed date formats (`YYYY-MM-DD` and `DD/MM/YYYY`) | Parsed both formats into a single `datetime` type | ~400 cells |
| Negative/zero quantities (data-entry errors) | Converted to absolute value; zeros set to 1 | 4 rows |
| Statistical outliers in Sales/Quantity | **Flagged** (not deleted) via IQR method (1.5×IQR), so the business can decide case-by-case | 50 values |
| Sales inconsistent with Qty × Price × (1-Discount) | **Flagged** as a validation/integrity check | 37 rows |

**Design choice:** outliers and validation mismatches were *flagged*, not silently dropped — in a real business dataset, a $30,000 order could be a legitimate bulk purchase, not an error. The cleaned file (`data/sales_data_cleaned.csv`) keeps `Sales_Outlier`, `Quantity_Outlier`, and `Sales_Validation_Flag` boolean columns so downstream analysis can filter them in or out as needed.

## 3. Correlation Analysis

| | Sales | Profit | Quantity | Discount | UnitPrice |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Sales** | 1.00 | 0.54 | 0.10 | -0.07 | 0.40 |
| **Profit** | 0.54 | 1.00 | 0.13 | -0.30 | 0.54 |
| **Discount** | -0.07 | **-0.30** | -0.03 | 1.00 | -0.02 |

**Key finding:** Discount has a **moderate negative correlation with Profit (-0.30)** and an even stronger negative correlation with profit margin (-0.55). Discounting is eroding margin faster than it's driving incremental volume (Quantity correlates only 0.10 with Sales) — worth a closer look at discount policy.

## 4. Trend & Breakdown Analysis

- **Best month:** August 2025 · **Weakest month:** May 2025. Monthly sales are volatile (see `visuals/01_monthly_sales_trend.png`) with no strong seasonal pattern — expected for synthetic sample data, but the analysis pipeline (`OrderMonth` grouping) is ready to reveal real seasonality once live data is loaded.
- **By Region:** South ($1.88M) and East ($1.83M) lead; North is lowest ($1.61M) — an 18% gap between top and bottom region.
- **By Category:** Fairly balanced — Office Supplies ($2.47M, 35%), Technology ($2.33M, 33%), Furniture ($2.27M, 32%).
- **Top products by revenue:** Desk, Paper, Monitor, Pens (Box), Binder.
- **Shipping performance:** Average shipping time is ~4.0 days across all ship modes, with no meaningful difference between "Same Day" and "Standard Class" in this sample — a red flag worth investigating in a real dataset, since it suggests `ShipMode` may not be operationally meaningful or the shipping-date data needs auditing.

---

## 5. Business Insights Summary

1. **Revenue is healthy but concentrated**: a right-skewed sales distribution means a relatively small number of large orders drive a disproportionate share of revenue — worth identifying and protecting those key accounts/products.
2. **Discounting is hurting margin more than it's helping volume.** Discount correlates weakly with Quantity (+0.10) but negatively with Profit (-0.30). Recommend testing a lower maximum discount tier.
3. **Regional gap:** North underperforms South by ~18% in revenue with a similar order count — worth investigating regional pricing, marketing spend, or customer mix.
4. **Category mix is well balanced** — no single category is a single point of failure, which is a resilience positive.
5. **Data quality flags found (50 outliers, 37 validation mismatches) should be reviewed** with the source system owner before this data is used for financial reporting — cleaning got the dataset analysis-ready, but a few rows still warrant a human look.
